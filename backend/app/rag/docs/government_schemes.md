# Municipal Citizen Welfare Schemes & Citizen Benefits (2026)

## 1. Clean City & Zero-Waste Housing Rebate
Housing societies and individual homes that implement 100% wet-waste composting and dry-waste recycling are eligible for a **15% Property Tax Rebate**.
- Application Process: File via CivicFlow AI RAG Portal under "Green Housing Subsidy".
- Inspection Timeline: 7 business days by Sanitation Officer.

## 2. Rainwater Harvesting Subsidy Scheme
The Municipal Corporation offers up to **₹50,000 or 50% cost subsidy** (whichever is lower) for installing rooftop rainwater harvesting systems in residential properties above 200 sq. meters.
- Requirements: Approved plumbing blueprint, proof of property ownership.

## 3. Solar Roof Light Incentives
Commercial and residential buildings installing solar panels on rooftops receive priority grid-connection and a **20% property tax reduction** for 3 consecutive years.

## 4. Senior Citizen Free Civic Mobility Pass
Citizens aged 60 and above receive complimentary access to municipal public electric bus services and free monthly health checkups at Ward Health Centers.
