# CivicFlow AI - Official Municipal Handbook & SLA Policy (2026 Edition)

## 1. Department SLA (Service Level Agreements) & Resolution Timelines

The Municipality mandates strict resolution timelines for all filed civic complaints:

- **Sanitation & Garbage Management:**
  - Standard Collection & Overflowing Bins: 24 Hours
  - Illegal Hazardous Waste Dumping: 12 Hours
  - Dead Animal Removal: 6 Hours

- **Roads & Infrastructure:**
  - Minor Pothole Filling: 48 Hours
  - Severe Structural Road Failure / Sinkhole: 12 Hours (Emergency Response)
  - Broken Footpaths / Paver Blocks: 72 Hours

- **Water Supply & Sewerage:**
  - Main Water Pipeline Leakage: 12 Hours
  - Contaminated Water Supply: 24 Hours
  - Overflowing Manhole / Sewer Line: 12 Hours

- **Electrical & Streetlighting:**
  - Individual Unlit Streetlight: 36 Hours
  - High-Voltage Wire Snap / Cable Hazard: 2 Hours (Urgent Hazard)
  - Dark Corridor (Whole Street Off): 12 Hours

- **Parks & Horticulture:**
  - Fallen Tree Blocking Road: 4 Hours
  - Dangerous Overhanging Branch: 24 Hours
  - Overgrown Grass / Maintenance: 7 Days

- **Traffic & Signals:**
  - Non-functional Traffic Signal: 6 Hours
  - Missing Road Signage / Barrier: 24 Hours
